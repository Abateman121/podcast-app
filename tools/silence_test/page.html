What to listen for

  1. Play two or three minutes with the switch off, then on. Flip it while it plays. The orange lamp is lit while a pause is being sped up.
  2. Clicks, pops, or a "chirp" at the start or end of a pause.
  3. Clipped words: the last sound of a sentence or the first sound of the next one is rushed or cut.
  4. Speech that sounds breathless or unnatural, compared with Overcast.
  5. Music and applause should play normally.
  6. Jump around in the episode, then listen again. If it gets worse after a jump with "Original" and is fine with "AAC copy", note that.
  7. On the phone: lock the screen with the box above ticked and unticked.
  8. Try it at your usual speed, in Chrome and in Safari.

Starting values from the spec: quieter than -35 dB, pause speed 3x, 80 ms untouched at each edge. If another setting sounds better, tell me which one.
